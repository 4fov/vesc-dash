Charge is estimated from pack voltage and the series cell count in Settings. Voltage sags under load, so it reads low while accelerating and recovers when you stop. A VESC cannot report individual cell voltages.

Range = battery capacity × charge left (estimated from pack voltage) ÷ your recent Wh per km, counting 0% at the voltage-curve floor. The bike may cut out earlier, and hard riding lowers the number. Set battery capacity in Settings. The map needs internet for tiles; the track still draws offline.
